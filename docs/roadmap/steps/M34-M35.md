## M34 · The Hundred Lamps, Hoot's Margins and the last gem sinks

**Goal.** Ship the last optional dungeon (the Hundred Lamps, ten floors that remix every mechanic, ending in the
Three Corners Jamboree), Hoot's notebook realm with his last paper, and every remaining gem sink (24 Nook furnishings,

`docs/roadmap/briefs/batch-15.md` written; `progress.md` entry for M34; `tests/economy.test.js` reports shipped

#### Why
Roadmap item **M34 · The Hundred Lamps, Hoot's Margins and the last gem sinks**. The Hundred Lamps is the post-game's
last optional dungeon: ten floors under Tower Head where every floor asks Pip to kindle ten lamps using one mechanic
he already knows, with harder numbers and later-land enemies, and **no new rules**. It is the game's "final exam" for
the whole toolset (CANON §9.2) and holds the 24th heart petal. This step builds the dungeon frame (per-level legends,
lamp counting, hatches, light that rises as lamps are lit, floor hints, the Vault door) and floors 1-5.

     `needsOnEntry: ['lantern', 'glide', 'tide', 'hook', 'sunglass', 'dig', 'starsong', 'sling', 'climb', 'cloudstep']`.
