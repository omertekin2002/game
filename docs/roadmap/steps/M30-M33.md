## M30 · The Starstair, Driftholm and the end of Act III

**Goal.** Pip climbs the restored Starstair from the Cairn's needle to Driftholm, the floating meadow where Mote has
lived alone for a hundred years; reads his Tally Wall; herds the Nimbles; climbs the Spiral Stair on Starsong clouds;
keeps up with Mote in the chase; and watches him pull the Ladder of Lights up through the Spiral Arch. Act III ends.

**Entry criteria.** M29 closed (S-261 stacked or merged). On a fixture at the end of S-256 (`stones==3`,
`memories==12`), the Stairfoot (S-143) stands inert on the needle, three Starstair flights hang over the Cairn (S-212),
and the Ivy Gloves (S-248) and Thistle Hook (S-143) exist.

**Exit criteria (what the player can do).** From the Stairfoot, `[E] Climb · the Starstair` (reachable by the two
hooks or by the new ivy route) carries Pip up three flights to Driftholm's Stair Landing. Driftholm is unhidden: four
regions, Mote's empty shelter, a hundred scrap lamps, the Tally Wall (side story 45), the Nimbles and Nimble Herding
(side story 46), 8 chests, 4 keepsakes, 6 acorns, 2 vistas, 4 benches, 10 curios, the Wind-Chime Hollow, the tenth
and last Dash course, Nimbus Puffs and Gust Kites. Cloudstep exists (found in the Spiral Stair's room 6) and opens the
home sky nook and Driftholm's mote routes. The Spiral Stair (d10) has 10 rooms on Starsong clouds and ends in the
chase; under the Spiral Arch Mote says "You saw it, didn't you." and the card "End of Act Three · The Remembering"
shows. `act3done` is set; the Spiral Arch shows `[E] Enter · the Spiral Arch` (locked with a line until M31 unhides the
Starmeadow).

**Play added.** +4.5 h (cumulative 78.5 h): the climb and arrival 0.3 h, Driftholm exploration and finds 1.5 h, side
story 45 0.25 h, side story 46 0.35 h, the Driftholm Dash 0.2 h, the Wind-Chime Hollow 0.2 h, the Spiral Stair 1.5 h,
the chase and the arch 0.2 h.

**Save plan.** No version bump: the save stays **v11**. Everything new is in `flags` (`stairfootReached`, `ch18`,
`ch18.tally`, `moteChase`, `act3done`, `ss45.*`, `ss46.*`, `scene:*`, `seen:*`, `gate:driftholm:*`), `items`
(`cloudstep`), `abilities` (`cloudstep`), `chests`, `secrets`, `collect` (`acorn`, `vista`, `hollow`, `petal`, `dash`),
`travel`, `bestiary`, `dungeons.spiral_stair` and `bosses['spiral_stair:mote']`.

**Realms.** New: `driftholm` (overworld, floating, 448 m, seed 40283), `spiral_stair` (dungeon d10),
`hollow_driftholm_1`. All three are `hidden: true` until **S-272**. New in-page suite: `sky` (S-264).

## Shared rules for every step in this file

Each step's Acceptance criteria say "**Invariants block**": paste the block from
[charter.md#invariants-block](../bible/charter.md#invariants-block) word for word, deleting only the lines that cannot
apply (a docs step drops the budget lines). The rules below are referenced as R1-R12.

<a id="r1"></a>**R1 Where things live.** Text: `js/text/en/<realm>.js` (`home`, `driftholm`, `spiral_stair`,
`starmeadow`, `ladder`, `crown`, `night`, `dreams`), loaded with the realm; text ids follow
[story.md#conventions](../bible/story.md#conventions) (`<realm>.<group>.<key>.<n>`). Dialogue entries:
`js/content/dialogue/<realm>.js`. Scenes: `js/content/scenes/<realm>.js`. Quests: `js/content/quests/<realm>.js`
(main quests `mq-*` live in the realm where their chapter starts). New flags: one entry each in
`js/content/flags.js` (`kind` per [systems.md#conditions](../bible/systems.md#conditions)). New items: `js/items.js`
`ITEMS`. NPC looks: `js/content/looks.js` `NPC_LOOKS`. Realm registry: `js/realms/index.js` `REALMS`. Realm module
(music, ambience, palette hooks, world states): `js/realms/<realm>.js`. If an earlier step put one of these in a
different file, use that file and say so in the PR body.

<a id="r2"></a>**R2 Flag spelling.** Where bible files disagree on a flag key, the spelling an earlier step already
shipped wins: memory flags are `mem:<n>` (M21-M23 reconciliation R3, set by S-190/S-212). Otherwise
[systems.md#flags](../bible/systems.md#flags) wins: freed Ladder lights `ladder:<light>`, the 13th letter's picks
`letter.1` … `letter.3`, the Planting choice `planting`. Derived counts are atoms in `js/cond.js`: `memories` (number
of `mem:1..12` set; shipped by S-190) and `freed` (number of `ladder:duskwick|pinewhistle|archwater|threemills`
set; S-280 adds it: pure, under 10 lines, unit-tested in `tests/cond.test.js`). Story.md's `freed:<isle>` and
`letter:<n>` mean these keys.

<a id="r3"></a>**R3 Quest ids.** Main quests use systems.md's ids `mq-ch18` … `mq-ch22`, with the step tables of
story.md (journal text, done condition, hint pair copied verbatim). Each step's id is given in its spec.

<a id="r4"></a>**R4 Hidden realms and portals.** A portal whose target realm is missing from `REALMS` or has
`hidden: true` is shown but **locked**: Use shows its `locked.say` line and does nothing else, unless
`G.debug.allowHidden === true` (tests set it; `?realm=<id>` and `G.debug.realm(id, spawn)` ignore `hidden` as before).
If S-021/S-026 did not add `allowHidden`, S-264 adds it (portal runtime: one condition; debug: one field). Unhide
schedule in these milestones: `driftholm`, `spiral_stair`, `hollow_driftholm_1` in S-272; `starmeadow` in S-278b;
`ladder` and `crown` in S-286b; `night` in S-290; each `dream_*` in the step that ships it.

<a id="r5"></a>**R5 Test suites.** Unit tests in `bun test` (`tests/*.test.js`). In-page suites are files in
`js/debug/tests/` run by `node tests/inpage.mjs <suite>`, each a matrix entry in `.github/workflows/ci.yml` capped at
15 min (shard `<suite>:k/n` past that). New suites here: `sky` (S-264: the Starstair, Driftholm, Cloudstep, the
Starmeadow, the Twinklings, the chase), `finale` (S-284: the Crown and ch21), `postgame` (S-288a: the Night, ch22,
the letter, side stories 48/53/14), `dreams` (S-292). Species tests go in the per-species in-page suite S-041 created;
dungeon puzzle tests in the dungeon suite S-052 created; realm budgets in the `realm` suite (S-022), which picks up
every `REALMS[id].views` automatically. Chapter tests: `node tests/chapter.mjs <id>` with
`tests/fixtures/saves/<id>-start.json` and `tests/fixtures/chapters/<id>.json` (S-030); the chain unit test checks each
chapter's end fixture against the next chapter's start fixture.

<a id="r6"></a>**R6 Machine.** Before any browser run: `free -h` shows at least 6 GB available; one browser and one
suite at a time; stop every browser and server started. `bun test` stays under 3 min.

<a id="r7"></a>**R7 Generated 2D art.** The implementing agent starts a GPT Sol subagent through the T3 orchestrator's
`delegate_task` tool and asks it to generate the image with Codex's built-in `image_gen.imagegen` (a Gemini model is
the fallback). The prompt is the house preamble of [art-audio.md#preamble](../bible/art-audio.md#preamble) + the kind
suffix of [art-audio.md#kinds](../bible/art-audio.md#kinds) + the subject line given in the step. Raw generations are
never committed: save them in a scratch folder, process them with the repo tools
([art-audio.md#pipeline](../bible/art-audio.md#pipeline): `tools/make_seamless.py`, `tools/slice_sheet.py`,
`tools/process_strip.py`, `tools/export_sprites.py`, the white-to-alpha one-liner), save webp at the size and byte
limit given, add the key to `assets/manifest.js` with `realm: '<id>'` (and `deferred: true` for realm art), and log
the prompt and commands in `docs/art/prompts.md`. Every key keeps the fallback given; `?noassets=all` must still
render. Images are original: never name or imitate another game, film, studio or artist.

<a id="r8"></a>**R8 Code-built 3D and ART BRIEFs.** New 3D is code-built in the toon style (`js/models.js`,
`js/props.js`, `js/toon.js`, realm prop files) and ships an ART BRIEF in the PR body using
[art-audio.md#brief-template](../bible/art-audio.md#brief-template) (every field filled). The briefs given compressed
in the bible are expanded to the template. Pip's GLB is never replaced. Glow uses `glow` / `nightGlow` material inputs
and additive sprites, never new point lights (the shipped 4-light pool stays).

<a id="r9"></a>**R9 Tone and first person.** [charter.md#tone](../bible/charter.md#tone): enemies poof; Pip gets
dizzy; nothing earned is taken back; nothing is missable; lines ≤ 220 characters; `~` only in Miso's speech, `!!` only
in Kinako's; Pip never speaks; only the characters charter decision 5 names say "Wenna"; the UI says "the Great Tree".
Every off-camera world change in a scene has a sound twin (story.md scene rule 6). Paste the tone checklist of
[charter.md#tone-check](../bible/charter.md#tone-check) into every PR that adds text or behaviour.

<a id="r10"></a>**R10 Pip never hits Mote.** From S-271 on, Mote is never a lock-on target; a swing that passes
through him plays a soft chime and does nothing. From `act3done` until `mainEnding` (Act IV), Pip's attack input is
ignored while Mote is within 12 m (story.md decision 27). The rule lives in one helper, `combat.moteLock(game)`, with
a unit test.

<a id="r11"></a>**R11 Size.** Each PR stays under about 500 changed lines excluding generated images, fixtures and
lockfiles. Steps already split here say so. If a step still runs long, ship part `a`, hand the rest back in a 🤖
comment, and the planner files part `b` with the same id (INDEX §1.6).
