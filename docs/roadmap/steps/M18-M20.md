# Steps M18-M20: Threemills stories and Village Works, the Lamplighters' Tower, and Act II aftercare

Issue-ready specs for S-152 to S-173, plus the splits listed in [Index changes](#index-changes). Each `### S-###`
block is pasted into one `agent:improvement` issue. The issue title is the heading text after `S-### · `, verbatim.
The `#### Why / What / Acceptance criteria / Verification plan / Out of scope / Risk & rollback` blocks become the
issue's `## ` sections; `#### Assets` goes inside `## What`. Bible links are relative to `docs/roadmap/steps/`
(`../bible/<file>.md#<anchor>` = `docs/roadmap/bible/<file>.md#<anchor>` in the repo).

Order of authority: `docs/roadmap/CANON-NOW.md` LOCKED > `docs/roadmap/CANON.md` > the reconciliation
rows in `docs/roadmap/steps/*.md` (for the topics they name, until the named close-out edits the losing file) > the
bible (`docs/roadmap/bible/*.md`) > the INDEX row > the issue. Where two
bible files disagree, these steps pick one reading. Every pick is in the [reconciliation table](#reconcile); the
step that ships a pick edits the losing bible file in the same PR (one or two lines), so the bible stays the single
source of truth, and the milestone close-out (S-159, S-166, S-173) checks that it was done.

## Conventions every step below assumes

These were built by earlier steps. The names are the bible's; **before coding, grep for the shipped name** (for example
`grep -rn "placeIsleStories\|storyMarks" js/`) and use what is really there. If a helper a step names does not exist,
the step builds the smallest version it needs and says so in the PR body.

- **Save.** The save is **v9** (S-071) for all of M18-M20; no step here bumps it. New state goes only in `S.flags`,
  `S.items`, `S.quests`, `S.collect`, `S.chests`, `S.secrets`, `S.bosses`, `S.plushies`, `S.rematches`, `S.travel`,
  `S.bestiary`, `S.dungeons`, with the id rules of [../bible/systems.md#ids](../bible/systems.md#ids). Every new flag key
  or family is registered in `js/content/flags.js` in the same PR (the content lint fails otherwise). Flag keys match
  `^[A-Za-z0-9:_.-]{1,48}$`.
- **Realms and content files.** `js/realms/index.js` (`REALMS`, `hidden`), `RealmManager.enter`, `G.debug.realm(id,
  spawn)`, `?realm=<id>`. Conditions and effects: `js/cond.js`. Text: `js/text/en/<file>.js` through `t(id)`; side-story
  lines in `js/text/en/side-<realm>.js` with ids `side.ssNN.<speaker>.<n>` ([../bible/side-content.md#data-shape](../bible/side-content.md#data-shape)).
  Dialogue: `js/content/dialogue/<realm>.js`. Scenes: `js/content/scenes/<realm>.js` run by `js/scenes.js`. Quests:
  `js/content/quests/<realm>.js` and `js/quests.js` (quest numbers `n`: side story `ss-NN` is `100 + NN`, activities
  201-212). Items: `js/items.js`. Gates and abilities: `js/gates.js`, `js/abilities.js`, solver `js/progress-graph.js`.
  Dungeons: `js/realms/dungeons/<id>.js`, `js/realms/dungeon-compile.js` (compiler + lock-and-key solver),
  `js/realms/recipes/dungeon.js`, `js/dungeon-mod.js`, `js/props-interior.js`. Interiors: S-054's interior recipe and
  `js/props-interior-home.js`. Bosses: `js/boss/defs.js` and the move runner (S-043-S-045). NPC looks: `NPC_LOOKS` in
  `js/content/looks.js`, one `npcDef` case per rig family in `js/models.js`. Economy: `js/content/economy.js` and
  `tests/economy.test.js` (S-078). Completion: `js/realms/stats.js` written by `tools/realm-stats.mjs` (S-074).
- **Isle post-passes.** Every new thing placed on an isle goes in that isle's `placeIsleStories` list
  (`new RNG(seed * 73 + 7)`, writes `W.storyMarks`), `placeIsleDash` (`seed * 73 + 3`) or `placeIsleRook`
  (`seed * 73 + 5`) ([../bible/atlas-isles.md#passes](../bible/atlas-isles.md#passes)). Lists are append-only, the
  pass runs after the realm's prints are taken, so `tests/fixtures/realm-prints/<id>.json` never changes. Threemills'
  seed is 40237, Duskwick's 40211.
- **Home post-passes.** Rows of [../bible/atlas-home.md#passes](../bible/atlas-home.md#passes): `placeSpots` (row 6,
  `7151 * 83 + 29`), `placeCourses` (row 11, `107, 47`), `placeVillageWorks` (row 15, `131, 67`), `placeAcorns` (row 16,
  `137, 71`), `placeOldBeacons` (row 17, `139, 73`). This file claims **row 27 `placeSlowWalk` = `new RNG(7151 * 211 +
  139)`** for S-172 (checked: no collision with any listed stream for seeds 1-200). The next pairs are already
  reserved: row 28 `(223, 151)` for S-212a's `placeStoneRing`, row 29 `(227, 157)` for S-215's `placeLetterboxes`;
  next free `(229, 163)`. Solids and decks are attached by `attachPost(W)` after `generateWorld` returns.
- **Players are in first person.** Every NPC who "follows" in these steps walks **ahead** of Pip with the guide module
  that S-152a adds ([R12](#reconcile)); every cue has a sound and a visual twin; every aimed thing shows a reticle
  change on target ([../bible/charter.md#first-person](../bible/charter.md#first-person)).
- **Text.** Lines are copied **verbatim** from the bible where it gives them. Lines this file writes are marked
  *(new)*; they obey [../bible/charter.md#voice](../bible/charter.md#voice): ≤ 220 characters, `...` not `…`, `~` only
  in Miso's lines, `!!` only in Kinako's, Pip never speaks, the speaker's tic in at least half their lines.
- **Art.** Painted images are generated inside the step by a GPT Sol subagent (T3 orchestrator `delegate_task`, Codex
  `image_gen.imagegen`) or a Gemini model, with the preamble of [../bible/art-audio.md#preamble](../bible/art-audio.md#preamble)
  + the kind suffix of [../bible/art-audio.md#kinds](../bible/art-audio.md#kinds) + the subject given in the step. Raw
  generations are never committed; the prompt and the processing command are appended to `docs/art/prompts.md`.
  Portraits follow [../bible/cast.md](../bible/cast.md)'s recipe (`tools/export_sprites.py`, 512² webp ≤ 60 KB).
  Every key is registered in `assets/manifest.js` with its `realm` tag and has the fallback named. 3D is code-built
  (`js/models.js`, `js/props.js`, `js/toon.js`) with the ART BRIEF pasted into the PR body in the form of
  [../bible/art-audio.md#brief-template](../bible/art-audio.md#brief-template); GLB slots are added to
  `assets/manifest.js` **commented out** with their height.
- **Invariants.** Every step's acceptance criteria include the block in
  [../bible/charter.md#invariants-block](../bible/charter.md#invariants-block), pasted word for word. Each step says
  which lines it keeps ("Invariants: keep all" or "keep all except ..."). The PR body carries the
  [tone checklist](../bible/charter.md#tone-check).
- **Machine.** `free -h` first (want ≥ 6 GB available); one browser at a time; stop every server and browser started.
  In-page runs are `node tests/inpage.mjs <suite>`; a new suite is a file `js/debug/tests/<name>.js` registered in
  `js/debug/tests.js` (the `register(suite)` pattern) and one entry in `.github/workflows/ci.yml`'s in-page matrix.
  Chapter runs are `node tests/chapter.mjs <id>`.
- **Standard verification** for every step (each step adds its own lines): `bun test` ·
  `node tools/build-pages.mjs && bun run test:pwa` (when assets or site files change) · `bun run test:smoke`.

**Goal.** Tool 5 and the end of Act II: the Sunglass (an aimed light beam), Sleeping Acorns that wake on every shipped

**Entry criteria.** M18 closed (S-159 stacked or merged). `towerOpen` reachable in the act2 order chapter tests; the
Glowbud Lantern, the dungeon engine with puzzle entities I and II (S-052, S-053), the progression solver (S-048, S-108),

**Exit criteria.**

  suite's 4 views in both cameras, and the map test (S-049) pass.
- The Wickwork Moth's balance test passes (careful bot ≥ 90 % wins in 120-180 s losing ≤ 3 hearts with 6 hearts and

  `beacons == 4`, `lanterns == 4`, items `sunglass` and the heart container).
- Sleeping Acorns wake on home, Duskwick, Pinewhistle, Archwater and Threemills (the `sunglass` in-page suite wakes
  one per land); ring 1 appears on the Tree at 25 woken; the Completion page's acorn rows count.
- `docs/DESIGN.md` says Act II is complete; `README.md` hours updated; `progress.md` entry for M19.

**What the player can do at the end.** Climb the Lamplighters' Tower floor by floor, bonking brass mirrors to bounce
skylight beams into brass sunflowers, find the Sunglass on floor 3 and use it to shine their own beam, win a heart
container, dazzle and calm the Wickwork Moth, kindle the great lamp and watch the Firstlight wake, then watch Mote take
it, crying sparks, and climb into the north-east sky, where a ladder of lights now hangs over every land. Back home the

#### Why
Roadmap item **M19 · The Lamplighters' Tower and the end of Act II** (`Roadmap: docs/roadmap/INDEX.md S-162`, part
a; split, see [Index changes](#index-changes)). The Tower's signature mechanic is light bounced by brass mirrors. Before
any Tower room is authored, the compiler, the lock-and-key solver and the runtime learn beams, so the Tower's maps are
proven solvable in `bun test` before a browser ever opens.

#### What
1. **Legend and entities** (dungeons.md's table rows for `/` `\` `m`, `S`, `R`; d5's legend extension):
   - `S` **beam source**: a fixed skylight collar (`int_skylight`) shining a beam along `dir` (`'N'|'E'|'S'|'W'`, from
     the entity table).
   - `/`, `\` **mirror** in a reflecting state; `m` **mirror** in a blocking state whose start is given as `'|'` (N-S
     blocking) or `'-'` (E-W blocking) in its entity. One sword hit turns it 45° clockwise in 0.25 s with a click:
     `/` → `|` → `\` → `-` → `/`. Reflection: `/` turns N→E, E→N, S→W, W→S; `\` turns N→W, W→N, S→E, E→S; `|` and `-`
     stop the beam. State saved in `S.dungeons[id].state['<room>:<glyph><n>']` (for example `'r3:m1': '/'`).
   - `R` **receiver** (`int_receiver`, a 1.3 m brass sunflower): lit while a beam enters its tile; acts like a plate
     (live) on doors, hatches and cages unless its entity says `latch: true` (then the first light opens its target for
     good, saved in `open`).
   - **Hatch**: a `^` whose entity has a condition (`lit: [ids]`, `latch: '<R id>'`, `locked: true`): a wooden trapdoor
     lid over the stairs that slides open (0.6 s) when the condition first holds, for good (saved). `locked` takes a small
     key (`[E] Unlock · Hatch`).
   - Beams pass over Pip and enemies (never blocked by them); walls `#`, pillars `I`, closed doors and closed cages block.
2. **Pure trace** `traceBeams(room, state)` in `js/realms/dungeon-compile.js`: from every source, step tile by tile in its
   direction, reflect on mirrors, stop on blockers or after 30 tiles; return the set of lit receiver ids and the beam
   polylines (tile centres). Used by both the solver and the runtime (one source of truth).
3. **Pip's own beam in a dungeon.** In rooms whose def lists beam entities, Pip's Sunglass beam (S-160) is **snapped to the
   nearest cardinal direction** when the view yaw is within 12° of one (a faint grid-aligned guide line shows the snapped
   path), so it enters the same tile trace: the runtime calls `traceBeams` with an extra source at Pip's tile and
   direction, reach **9 tiles** (18 m). Outside 12° the beam is the free S-160 beam (it lights receivers it touches, but
   does not reflect). A Sunglass hit on a mirror does not turn it (only the sword does).
4. **Solver.** `solve()` adds mirror states to the search state and two free actions in a reachable room: "hit mirror"
   (cycle one step) and, once `sunglass` is held, "beam from tile t in direction d" for every reachable floor tile with a
   clear line (9 tiles); receivers, hatches, latches and cages are evaluated with `traceBeams`. Latched targets join the
   persistent `open` set. Cap the state space with a visited hash on (room, keys, bigKey, opened set, mirror states).
   Report: the action list of the shortest solution (printed on failure).
5. **Runtime** (`js/dungeon-mod.js`): build sources, mirrors (turntable posts with a brass-framed disc `#cfe3ee`) and
   receivers from the compiled data; render every beam as additive ribbons (`fx_beam`, 0.25 m wide, ≤ 1 draw per room
   through one dynamic buffer); recompute `traceBeams` on any mirror change, door change or Pip-beam change (not every
   frame); receivers' petals open (gold `#f2b45a`) while lit, emit `EV.PUZZLE`; sound `sfx.receiverOn` (a warm chord) and
   `sfx.mirrorClick`. First-person readability (dungeons R7): every beam room's source, mirrors and receiver are visible
   from its entry door.
6. **Fixture dungeon** `tests/fixtures/dungeons/beam_test.js` (two rooms): room A, a source shining east onto a `/` that
   must become `\` to reach a latch receiver that opens the door to room B; room B, a receiver reachable only by Pip's own
   beam from one tile; and a second fixture `beam_softlock.js` where the only receiver is behind a pillar from every
   reachable tile (must fail with a readable message).

#### Assets
- `int_mirror` (code-built, ≤ 300 tris: a 1.2 m round brass-framed mirror on a turntable post, glass `#cfe3ee`),
  `int_skylight` (≤ 120 tris: brass collar + a gold light-cone card), `int_receiver` (≤ 400 tris: a 1.3 m brass sunflower
